# DOU product video scripts

## Arabic master video — 8 to 10 minutes

### 00:00–00:35 — Introduction
**Visual:** DOU sales page, then the client login.
**Voice-over:** «مرحبًا بكم في DOU، منصة إدارة السائقين للشركات اللوجستية. من مكان واحد تقدر تدير السائقين، الحضور، المستندات، المشاريع، التارجت، الحوافز والتقارير.»

### 00:35–01:30 — Dashboard
Show the daily summary, expiring documents, attendance, monthly orders and estimated payroll. State clearly that payroll values are estimates, not money transfers.

### 01:30–02:45 — Drivers
Add a driver. Show personal details, nationality, identity number, emergency contact, supervisor, project and vehicle. Open the profile and demonstrate a project transfer with history.

### 02:45–03:40 — Supervisors and permissions
Create a supervisor and demonstrate that they only see assigned drivers. Show custom permission checkboxes and project-manager scope.

### 03:40–04:35 — Attendance and documents
Switch to the driver app, check in with GPS and upload a document. Return to the company dashboard, approve the upload and show expiry alerts.

### 04:35–05:35 — Targets and incentives
Create a project target: target orders, target amount and excess-order rate. Log daily orders from the driver app, then show remaining target and red/green status in reports.

### 05:35–06:30 — Requests and tickets
Submit leave, advance, shift change, project transfer, maintenance and incident requests. Raise a support ticket, reply as the company and show the reply in the driver app.

### 06:30–07:30 — Reports
Filter by date, project, nationality, city and supervisor. Export daily achievement and monthly attendance to Excel.

### 07:30–08:15 — DOU administration
Show company activation, plans in SAR and USD, country, default language, currency, time zone, subscription payment and audit log.

### 08:15–End — Call to action
«ابدأ تجربة DOU لشركتك من dou.delivery أو تواصل معنا على sales@dou.delivery.»

## English master video — 8 to 10 minutes

### 00:00–00:35 — Introduction
**Visual:** English sales page and client login.
**Voice-over:** “Welcome to DOU, the driver workforce management platform for logistics companies. DOU brings drivers, attendance, compliance, projects, targets, incentives and reporting into one place.”

### 00:35–01:30 — Dashboard
Present daily operating metrics, compliance alerts, attendance, monthly orders and estimated payroll. Clarify that payroll figures are estimates, not accounting or payment transfers.

### 01:30–02:45 — Driver records
Create a driver and demonstrate identity, nationality, emergency, supervisor, project and vehicle fields. Open the profile and show project-transfer history.

### 02:45–03:40 — Roles and permissions
Create a supervisor and demonstrate scoped driver access. Show granular permissions and project-manager scope.

### 03:40–04:35 — Attendance and compliance
Use the driver app to check in with GPS and upload a document. Approve it in the company dashboard and review expiry alerts.

### 04:35–05:35 — Targets and incentives
Create a project target, log driver orders, then show remaining orders, achieved targets and excess-order incentives.

### 05:35–06:30 — Requests and tickets
Submit employee requests and a support ticket. Reply from the company dashboard and display the reply in the driver app.

### 06:30–07:30 — Reports
Use date, project, nationality, city and supervisor filters, then export daily and monthly reports.

### 07:30–08:15 — DOU administration
Show company activation, local and USD pricing, market, language, currency, time zone, payments and the audit trail.

### 08:15–End — Call to action
“Start your DOU demo at dou.delivery or contact sales@dou.delivery.”

## Production notes
- Record at 1920×1080, 30 fps.
- Produce separate Arabic and English recordings; do not use subtitles as a replacement for localized narration.
- Hide real phone numbers, identity numbers, tokens and customer data.
- Use the demo company and driver accounts only.
- Add chapter titles and on-screen highlights for every click.
